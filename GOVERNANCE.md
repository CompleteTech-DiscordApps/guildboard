# Governance

The currently known repository maintainer is @CompleteDotTech. No other contributors or approvers have been assigned. Functional Owner Role values identify the expertise needed, not staffing commitments. New maintainers require an explicit recorded appointment; CODEOWNERS must contain only actual known maintainers.

Contributors follow [the Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and report security issues privately as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers coordinate issue ownership, triage, reviews and acceptance, and resolve scope or sequencing disputes. Reviewers independently assess their assigned scope and evidence; expertise labels do not appoint people or create approvals. Required native approvals, protections and checks remain effective.

Propose significant architecture changes through an ADR with alternatives, contracts, security effects and consumer handoffs. Within an explicitly owner-authorized campaign, its coordinator and scoped agents decide ordinary architecture, security and consumer section matters within their assigned authority. The coordinator resolves cross-section decisions and records affected items, decision owner, action, rationale, requirements and evidence, validation and follow-through. A proposed ADR alone does not require a human decision. Escalate a specific requirement for an actual human decision, native approval or protection, production authorization, access, provider restriction or explicit STOP to the authorized person or mechanism; record the exact source and required action. Do not treat technical review as a native approval or claim missing runtime evidence.

Owner authorization for a campaign does not appoint maintainers or grant standing delegation, permissions or release authority. Review requirements are not waived by Project status or merged planning documents. Versions follow semantic versioning once release artifacts exist. MVP and v1.0 gates require evidence; no deadline is committed. Maintain a changelog and deprecation policy before stable release.
