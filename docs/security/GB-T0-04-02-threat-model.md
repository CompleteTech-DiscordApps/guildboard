| Trust crossing / attack | Required design mitigation | Negative verification and responsible follow-through |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discord / Activity to API: forged identity, guild, role or selector | Server verifies identity and trusted guild/membership evidence; scoped selectors never confer authority. Foreign and unknown boards have the same public outcome. | Manager: coordinate T1/T2/T8 tests of forged context, foreign and unknown selectors, and absence of protected data or mutation across HTTP, WSS and bot (R03/R04, UC05/UC06, I02/I11). | Session/policy to command: viewer or stale/revoked member writes | Recheck session, membership, grant, lock and policy at serialized write fence; unavailable trusted evidence fails closed. | Manager: coordinate T1/T2/T6/T8 tests of viewer forgery, stale membership, grant revocation and lock with no mutation, revision or accepted event (R02/R04/R06, UC04/UC06/UC07/UC15/UC19, I03/I11/I13). |
| Command to protected release: revoke races with queued events, receipts or chunks | Serialize read authorization at every protected release; revocation first suppresses later releases; bytes already emitted cannot be recalled. | Manager: coordinate T4/T8/T9 tests of expired session and read revocation during ACK, receipt, snapshot and asset delivery with no later protected bytes (R04/R05, UC08/UC09, I04/I05/I15). |
| Client/bot to collaboration: duplicate, conflicting, concurrent or interrupted intent | Shared server authority validates bounded intents, orders accepted operations and binds idempotency identity to digest and preconditions. | Private storage to recovery: old lineage replay or partial snapshot promotion | Preserve historical receipts without applying old operations to restored lineage; verify complete snapshot/digest and ordered tail before promotion. | Manager: coordinate T5/T12 restore, old-lineage and interrupted-snapshot tests (R05/R06, UC11/UC12/UC13/UC16, I08/I09). Missing recovery evidence never proves non-commit. |
| Upload/import to private assets: path traversal, unbounded or malicious content, partial finalization | Quarantine scoped uploads, bounded sizes and opaque references; validate and scan before atomic finalization and board reference; protect every delivery chunk.
