## Scope and criteria

Each requirement ID below has one or more fixtures; observable outcomes are acceptance criteria to be implemented and independently verified by consumers. Identity, membership, session, policy and board selectors are server verified. No client-supplied role or tenant establishes authority. Error names refer to the contract's logical schemas; actual versioned codecs and bounds remain downstream work.

- **R01 Workshop**: A verified moderator with current authority can lead a guild workshop, open its board and submit permitted bounded edits; other authorized moderators can edit simultaneously. Each accepted edit is sequenced and durably committed before its acknowledgment. Users see pending, accepted or rejected states rather than assuming a timeout succeeded.
- **R02 Viewer**: An authorized viewer can join and receive the current board and live authorized updates. Viewer participation is observation; presentation or follow mode grants no write permission. A forged write is denied without content mutation, revision or accepted event.
- **R03 Guild ownership**: A board belongs to one guild. Server-side verified guild and scoped read policy govern selection and every protected delivery; foreign and unknown resources have the same public not-found outcome. A selector or visible UI is not authority.
- **R04 Authority and abuse**: New writes require current verified identity, session, membership, policy, lock and grant at the serialized commit fence. Expired or revoked sessions/grants, stale membership, invalidated policy, lock or unavailable trusted prerequisites fail closed. Revocation ordered before a write prevents that write; a previously committed operation remains accepted. At every protected response or queued chunk release, current read authorization and freshness are checked; a prior revocation suppresses later releases, though emitted bytes cannot be recalled. Denials disclose no protected receipt or state and create no content side effects.
- **R05 Recovery**: On an unknown outcome, retry preserves operation identity, digest and preconditions. A matching retained receipt after current read checks resolves its historical commit without another mutation. Restore preserves receipts and creates a new lineage; a retained old receipt does not apply old content to the restored document. A receipt-free old-lineage intent is rejected and requires reconciliation and deliberate discard or new authorized intent. Missing evidence after disaster recovery does not establish non-commit. Reconnect reauthorizes and verifies complete snapshot/digest plus ordered tail, or bounded replay; interrupted snapshots are not promoted.
- **R06 Failure and configuration**: Invalid or missing configuration, unavailable required identity/policy/storage or trusted time, and interrupted execution fail closed with safe diagnostics. No secrets or unnecessary board content enter logs; no partial command is acknowledged as saved. Outcomes of already committed writes remain unknown until authorized recovery resolves them.
- **R07 Release scope**: MVP requires every integrated behavior in the [MVP matrix](../../planning/MVP-ACCEPTANCE.md), including Activity startup, guild context, moderator verification, editor, concurrent editing, viewer access, backend permissions, sync, saves/APIs, locking, reconnect and security negatives. Integrated acceptance #455 precedes MVP approval #456; a subset handoff states limitations. Stable v1.0 additionally requires all mandatory full-workstream gates, including production recovery/GoDaddy #457 and stable release #472; MVP demonstration alone does not establish those gates. Publication is separate (#471). No release is asserted.
- **R08 Objectives**: Evaluate the availability, capacity, latency and recovery targets below using their stated denominators and failure rules; publish actual evidence and unmet targets. They are test objectives, not measurements, a service-level agreement or capacity guarantee.

## Operational test objectives

The coordinator selected the system contract §7 baseline for this section; it remains reversible on evidence and does not change the contract or authorize deployment. T11/T12 must record actual server hardware, OS, runtime, database and storage configuration, topology, software revisions, dataset generator, client/load-generator placement, warm-up, clocks and measurement tooling before each run. Use a representative GoDaddy Linux VPS environment for production claims; report any other environment as such. Assets are excluded pending separate byte and decoded limits. Count attempts, errors, timeouts and invalid samples, with raw distributions and reasons; do not silently discard failures.

| Objective | Measurement and pass/fail |
| --- | --- |
| Capacity and durable ACK latency | Run 10 active boards, each with 20 connected participants, 5 simultaneous authorized writers and 5,000 supported elements; each writer submits 5 bounded operations/second for 30 minutes steady state (250 attempts/second aggregate), with actual mix, encoded sizes and conflict-free element allocation recorded. Server latency runs from complete validated command receipt at server ingress to durable ACK ready for release, using a monotonic clock; network and Discord/client latency are reported separately. Denominator is all valid submitted attempts in the window, including errors and timeouts; report successful durable ACK distribution and counts. Pass only if offered workload is sustained, every valid attempt receives its correct durable ACK within the observation window, no security/consistency failures occur, and p95 <=250 ms and p99 <=1 s among all attempts. An error, timeout, missing ACK, insufficient offered load or incomplete measurement fails; no percentile can hide failures. |
| Reconnect | Under the declared 5,000-element board and load above, disconnect and reconnect each of 20 participants per board once in a recorded distributed schedule; start at client reconnect initiation and stop when a complete authorized snapshot/replay and tail have been validated and current state is usable. Record snapshot bytes, RTT, disconnect schedule, concurrency and network path. Denominator is all 200 reconnect attempts; pass only if all complete <=5 s without partial promotion, missing accepted events or authorization leak. Timeout, denial of an otherwise authorized attempt, incomplete state or missing timing fails. Report separately from server ACK latency. |
| Process restart | On the same declared workload and environment, deliberately restart the service with intact durable storage at least once during load and once immediately after a recorded durable ACK. Compare every pre-restart acknowledged operation and receipt with recovered durable history, and verify state and lineage under the contract. Denominator is every acknowledged operation before each restart; pass only with zero lost acknowledged operations and correct recovery. Missing or unverifiable evidence fails; interrupted unacknowledged operations remain unknown until resolved. |
| Backup recovery | In a separately documented disaster-recovery exercise with production-like backup configuration, record the last durable commit before failure, latest recoverable backup point, failure time, restore start and time service and authorized board access are verified. RPO is elapsed time from recoverable point to failure, <=24 h; RTO is elapsed failure-to-verified-service time, <=4 h. Denominator is each planned restore exercise; at least one complete exercise is required, all must pass integrity and timing. Record actual data and receipt gaps; up to the RPO window may be lost and missing receipts do not prove non-commit. Untested, incomplete or unverifiable restore fails. Backup policy, retention and disaster reconciliation remain T5/T9/T11 work. |
| Availability | For each UTC calendar month, probe an authorized representative end-to-end board read and durable write at one-minute intervals from a documented independent vantage, with test data isolated and cleaned through authorized lifecycle; record probe design, maintenance and monitoring gaps. A minute is available only when both operations succeed within their declared timeouts; all scheduled minutes, including maintenance and missing/indeterminate probes, are the denominator. Pass when available minutes / total scheduled minutes >=99.5%; publish counts, incidents and gaps. This measurement needs deployed service, representative access and operational monitoring; one month cannot establish future availability. |

## Handoff and evidence

T1/T2/T6 resolve identity freshness and role/mode policy; T3/T4/T8 define supported operation schemas and collaboration behavior; T5/T9 own durability, retention and disaster reconciliation; T7 owns accessible states; T10/T11 own real VPS operations and measurements; T12 converts fixtures and objectives into integrated tests, and T13 records release evidence. Real Discord Activity/client/proxy, GoDaddy, database, browser, accessibility, security and license checks remain actual downstream work. No fixture, baseline or documentation check proves those checks passed. Versioned interfaces, bounded inputs and safe explicit errors must follow the system contract and downstream executable schemas. Independent review, native CI and configured protected merge, then post-merge verification remain required.
