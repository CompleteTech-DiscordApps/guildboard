# License and supply-chain policy — 2026-11-16

Decision owner: delegated #16 section owner. Decision: accept a conservative engineering policy for the current locked npm tooling only. MIT and Apache-2.0 can coexist with Guildboard MIT when their own terms and notices are preserved; third-party material is not relicensed. This is an engineering decision, not legal clearance. Evidence: [dated disposition](../research/license-adoption-evidence.md), [inventory](inventory.json), [original tarball ledger](../research/evidence/proposed-tooling-licenses-2026-10-24.json). The checker is an input consistency gate, not a license scanner or release approval.

Before adoption or distribution, inventory every direct and transitive package, build and runtime input, OS/container package, action, embedded material, font, icon and imported asset. Record exact version, source, artifact integrity, SPDX expression, complete notices and hashes, copyright, modifications, relationship, use, actual shipped paths, distribution modes and reviewer evidence. Unknown, custom, absent or conflicting terms, notices, origin, bytes or inventory block adoption and distribution until resolved and reviewed. A new or changed lock or workspace manifest must be reviewed and the inventory updated; the check detects changes, but cannot establish the truth of a deliberately altered inventory. Review lifecycle scripts, provenance and full dependency graph, compare notices and artifact hashes, review actual packaging, retain prior reviewed records for rollback, and seek independent review under repository governance. No automatic acceptance on upgrade.

## Distribution

Review source archives (including planning evidence), tooling packages or installed build environments, browser bundles and downloaded fonts, exported documents with or without embedded font software, and server/container images separately. Identify actual included bytes and make corresponding notices accessible in each distributed artifact; inspect resulting artifacts before release. A dev label does not determine whether a package or copied/generated material ships. Preserve MIT copyright and permission notices; for Apache-2.0 include license and applicable NOTICE/attribution, mark modified files and review patent/trademark terms. Preserve TypeScript LICENSE.txt and ThirdPartyNoticeText.txt for tooling distributions pending artifact-specific review. OFL-1.1 fonts require the full license/copyright, reserved-name and modification review, and restrictions on standalone sale; do not assume document export embeds or omits font software.

Fonts, icons and other imported assets have separate provenance and rights records; the historical Excalidraw observations do not adopt or clear them. User-provided assets remain separately owned and require a documented right to store, render and distribute for the applicable feature; an upload is not a grant to relicense. `planning/audit/project-overview.jpg` has unresolved rights: source archives containing it are blocked pending evidence. No browser bundle, export, font distribution or container exists to inspect here. Historical Excalidraw, SDK and font observations do not establish their adoption or rights.
